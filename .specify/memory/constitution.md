# Portfolio Constitution

1. Preserve the approved paper, graphite, lime palette and Manrope / Playfair Display / DM Mono typography.
2. Lead with the full story: question, idea, architecture, implementation, operation, and research relevance.
3. Ground facts in the supplied resume and project READMEs. User updates supersede stale counts. Never invent credentials, endorsements, fine-tuning results, or project screenshots.
4. Keep ObjectTracer, Second Brain and Wave Function Collapse distinct. Label architectural diagrams and active research accurately.
5. Put employer experience in the final chapter and identify Intel as project context and AtkinsRealis as employer.
6. Keep the static site usable from files or HTTP, accessible by keyboard, readable without JavaScript, and respectful of reduced motion.
7. Scope motion to the active viewport and avoid tracking, new backends, or unnecessary packages.
